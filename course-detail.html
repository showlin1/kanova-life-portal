COURSE HIGHLIGHTS

Why Complete This Certification?

Canadian Red Cross Certified

Earn a certified credential accepted across Canada. Gain genuine hands-on confidence to step up during unexpected emergencies.

WSIB Regulation 1101 Compliant

Meets mandatory Ontario workplace safety requirements for workplace first aid attendants in commercial and industrial settings.

Valid For 3 Full Years

Digital Red Cross certificate issued directly to your inbox within 24-48 business hours, valid nationwide for 3 full years.

Course Prerequisites

No prior first aid certification or medical training is required. This course is ideal for workplace designated first aiders, healthcare candidates, construction workers, teachers, and anyone seeking to learn essential life-saving skills.

What Will You Learn?

Delivered in accordance with the latest Canadian Red Cross and ILCOR emergency resuscitation guidelines:

  • ✔ CPR & AED for Adults, Children & Infants
  • ✔ Airway Obstruction & Conscious/Unconscious Choking
  • ✔ Cardiovascular Emergencies (Heart Attack, Stroke)
  • ✔ Bleeding Management, Tourniquet Application & Wound Dressing
  • ✔ Anaphylaxis, EpiPen & Auto-Injector Administration
  • ✔ Head, Neck & Spinal Immobilization Techniques
  • ✔ Bone, Joint & Muscle Injury Splinting
  • ✔ Environmental Illness, Hypothermia & Heat Stroke

Certification & Assessment

To receive your official Canadian Red Cross certification:

  • 100% attendance during in-person classroom instruction and practice.
  • Demonstration of all required practical resuscitation and first aid skills.
  • Achievement of a minimum score of 75% on the knowledge assessment quiz.
